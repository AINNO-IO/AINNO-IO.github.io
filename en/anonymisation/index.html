Features

Everything you need to publish documents safely

Many file formats

PDF (text and scanned), Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), JPG, JPEG and PNG images, scanned documents and drawings.

Large documents

Documents with hundreds of pages are supported. They are processed in the background: you see the progress and can keep working.

The app runs on your own computer or internal server and does not need an internet connection. Your documents stay with you.

Hidden text is deleted from the file together with the metadata (author, software). The result is verified – the data cannot be restored.

Installed on your own server, the app can be used securely by the whole organisation. A fixed monthly licence fee – with no limit on users or documents.

Drawings and plans

Not only A4: large architectural drawings and land plot plans, including scanned ones.

Signatures

Handwritten signatures are detected and covered automatically. Any other text or area you can hide yourself – just select it with the mouse.

Your own rules

Permit, certificate or contract numbers: enter an example or the exact text and create your own rules for the whole organisation.

How it works

Security and GDPR

Full privacy – in your own infrastructure

The app helps you meet GDPR requirements: documents you publish or share no longer contain excessive personal data, and the documents themselves are processed only on your server.

  • Installed on your server – Windows or Linux, users work in a browser.
  • No internet connection – the app also works in an isolated network.
  • Files are not kept – uploaded and result files are deleted automatically (default setting).
  • Organisation accounts – Active Directory / Entra ID or the app's own users.
  • Administration – settings, users and usage statistics in one place.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

There is a fixed monthly licence fee that does not limit the number of users or documents processed. The price is set individually – get in touch and we will send you an offer.

Does the app find 100% of personal data?

No, and no automatic tool promises that. That is why you can review the result before downloading, and hide any missed text or area simply by selecting it with the mouse.

Are documents sent to the cloud?

No. Your documents stay with you: everything happens on your server, and you can work without an internet connection.

Can hidden text be restored?

No. The text is deleted from the file, the metadata is cleaned, and the result is verified: if a hidden value can still be found, that page is redrawn as an image.

How large can documents be?

From a single page to hundreds of pages. Large drawings and scanned documents take longer than plain text.

How many users can work with it?

The licence does not limit the number of users or documents. Users sign in with organisation accounts or the app's own users.
